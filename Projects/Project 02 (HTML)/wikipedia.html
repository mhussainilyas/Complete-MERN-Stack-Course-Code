Here's a time-line of his life...

Dr. Abdul Qadeer Khan (A.Q. Khan) was a Pakistani metallurgical engineer and nuclear scientist who played a major role in developing Pakistan's uranium-enrichment program.Dr. A.Q. Khan was one of the most prominent scientists associated with Pakistan's nuclear program. He subsequently became an extremely well-known figure in Pakistan and was popularly called "Mohsin-e-Pakistan" (Benefactor of Pakistan).

  • 1936 — Dr. Abdul Qadeer Khan was born on 1 April 1936 in Bhopal, British India.
  • 1952 — He migrated to Pakistan with his family.
  • 1956 — He completed his studies in Metallurgical Engineering at the University of Karachi.
  • 1961 — He went to Europe for higher education.
  • 1967 — He earned an advanced degree in Metallurgical Engineering from Delft University of Technology in the Netherlands.
  • 1972 — He completed his PhD in Metallurgical Engineering from the Catholic University of Leuven, Belgium.
  • 1974 — After India's first nuclear test, he returned to Pakistan and became involved in Pakistan's nuclear program.
  • 1976 — He played a major role in establishing the Engineering Research Laboratories (ERL) at Kahuta, later known as Khan Research Laboratories (KRL).
  • 1998 — Pakistan conducted its nuclear tests at Chagai, Balochistan, on 28 and 30 May. Khan was one of the prominent scientists associated with Pakistan's nuclear program.
  • 1998 — He received the Nishan-e-Imtiaz, one of Pakistan's highest civilian awards.
  • 2021 — Dr. Abdul Qadeer Khan died on 10 October 2021 in Islamabad at the age of 85.
